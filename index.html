Ming-Chin Lin, MD, PhD

Neurosurgeon × Medical Informatics — AI-based neurological prognosis prediction

Ming-Chin Lin, MD, PhD, is an attending neurosurgeon at Wan Fang Hospital and Associate Professor at the Graduate Institute of Biomedical Informatics, Taipei Medical University. His work bridges the operating room and artificial intelligence: he leads a multicenter program applying multimodal machine learning to predict recovery in patients with disorders of consciousness, and has performed over 350 ROSA robotic-arm-assisted procedures. He currently serves as Vice President of the International Medical Informatics Association (IMIA, 2025–2027) and President of the Taiwan Association for Medical Informatics Education.
